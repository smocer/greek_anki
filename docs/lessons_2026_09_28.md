## Teaching decisions and Russian connections

- **Cases are practiced, not only described.** Compare ο γείτονας / τον γείτονα / του γείτονα, ο σκύλος / τον σκύλο / του σκύλου, το σπίτι / του σπιτιού / τα σπίτια. Russian «кто? / кого? / чей?» helps explain their roles, but matching Russian endings is not assumed.
- **Location and company:** Russian «в банке» is prepositional and «с Джулией» instrumental; Greek uses accusative after σε and με: στην τράπεζα, με την Τζούλια.
- **Gender and number:** Athens is η Αθήνα, singular despite Russian «Афины». Latsia is τα Λατσιά → στα Λατσιά. Nicosia is η Λευκωσία → στη Λευκωσία (the notebook's στην variant is accepted). Piraeus and Strovolos lose final ς after στον.
- **Ты/Вы:** every new verb drills both second-person forms. The phone cards contrast σου with σας; the subject pronoun is εσείς.
- **Possession:** Greek έχω often translates as Russian «у меня есть». It conjugates, unlike Russian «есть» in this construction. Δεν έχουμε corresponds naturally to «у нас нет».
- **Reading, learning and studying:** διαβάζω covers reading and studying/revising lessons; μαθαίνω is acquiring knowledge/skills or finding out; σπουδάζω normally means higher/specialist education. It is not a general label for any student doing homework.
- **Stress can distinguish words:** article η versus conjunction ή. Hard mode checks both. Τηλέφωνό μου/σου/σας needs the extra possessive stress, as does όνομά μου.
- **Genuine alternatives:** δεκάξι and δεκαέξι, εννέα and εννιά, οκτώ and οχτώ, εφτά and επτά, plural -ουν/-ουνε, ακόμα/ακόμη. Each accepted form keeps its own correct stress; no globally unaccented aliases were added. Existing primary εφτά is preserved.
- **Diminutives:** compare -άκι with Russian -ик/-очк-/-ёнок, but teach complete forms and the change to neuter το. Not every noun forms a diminutive by simply appending άκι.
- **Conversation register:** Χαίρετε is polite; Άντε γεια is familiar. Πω πω is surprise/dismay, not literally a reference to God. Καλησπέρα greets; Καλό βράδυ wishes someone a good evening when leaving.
- **Keyboard:** address numbers are written out in Greek. Every primary answer is enterable with the existing built-in letters and stress keys; sentence punctuation remains optional.
- **Pronunciation aids:** Latin and Cyrillic spellings are approximations. Russian lacks exact equivalents for θ, δ and some γ sounds; familiar roots help memory but do not guarantee identical pronunciation or meaning.

## Implementation and validation

New content is explicit, editable `const VocabularyDeck` data in `lib/data/decks/`, registered in `greek_decks.dart`. There are no runtime inflection rules or content editors. The existing session/observer and persistence/adapter boundaries already support new topics; adding more abstractions would not simplify this change.

Validation covers bilingual completeness, stable IDs, small topic sizes, all registered decks completing in hard mode, new six-person forms, noun-case rejection, spelling variants, required stress (including η/ή and double possessive accents), keyboard reachability, and existing progress carrying forward. Android is rebuilt; iOS remains unvalidated.
