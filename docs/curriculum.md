# Topics and learning guide

The collection contains **477 cards in 52 topics**, with 6–13 cards per topic. Every topic is available in English and Russian, in Browse, flashcards, and hard mode. The material covers beginner vocabulary and grammar; it is not a complete A1 course.

## Numbers

- Numbers 0–10
- Numbers 11–20
- Tens through 100
- Compound numbers, including examples through 101

## Conversations

- Greetings and courtesy
- How are you? — informal and polite/plural forms
- Introducing yourself
- Polite address — κύριος/κύριε, κυρία, φίλος/φίλε
- On the phone — answering a call, identifying yourself, asking who or where
- More greetings and farewells
- Small talk and how you feel

## Countries, residence, and places

- Countries and their grammatical gender
- Where are you from? — από + accusative
- Location — σε + article: στον / στην / στο
- Where I live — masculine, feminine, neuter, and plural place names
- Addresses and nearby places — exact location, near/far, streets, and addresses
- Here, there, and not here — είμαι, δεν, home, airport, and με + accusative
- Everyday places — airport, bank, university, and supermarket

## Classroom language and questions

- Classroom objects — articles, books, pages, exercises, and children
- Classroom phrases — instructions and asking for help
- Question words — including ποιος / ποια / ποιο

## Words and basic grammar

- Subject pronouns — gender, number, and εσύ / εσείς
- Articles and basic cases — nominative, accusative, genitive, and plurals
- My, your, our — possession in phrases
- Greek names — subject and direct-address forms
- Neighbours — noun cases and plurals
- Pets and home — dog, cat, house, and forms such as του σπιτιού
- Small things: -άκι — σκυλάκι, γατάκι, σπιτάκι, παιδάκι, νεράκι, καφεδάκι
- Connecting words and time
- Small words with different meanings — η/ή, δεν/όχι, πάντα, ακόμα/ακόμη, μήπως, με

## Present-tense verbs

These Greek forms mean “I am,” “I drink,” and so on. Modern Greek does not use an ordinary infinitive in the same way as English or Russian dictionary forms.

## Choosing your practice

Select **Choose a topic / Выбрать тему**, search by topic or word, then choose flashcards or hard mode. **Browse / Все слова** opens the vocabulary reference with examples and explanations. During practice, explanations appear after you reveal or check the answer.

Hard mode requires the correct Greek letters, stress marks, words, articles, and endings. Capitalization and sentence punctuation are optional. Topic search accepts queries without stress marks. Listed variants and accepted optional subject pronouns are also valid answers.
