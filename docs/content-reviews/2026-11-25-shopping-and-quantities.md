# Shopping vocabulary and quantity grammar — November 2026

Independent English, Russian and Chinese reviews cover 97 vocabulary cards, four practical expressions and 13 grammar exercises. Explanations distinguish grammatical gender, quantity agreement, countable portions of mass nouns and formal address. Existing vocabulary and review identities remain unchanged.

The following evidence supports the word-history and memory notes. Ancient forms are labelled and glossed; myths are cultural associations, not claims about the invention of a word or object. Ordinary usage examples are authored examples rather than attributed quotations.

## Grammar and answer design

- Quantity agreement: [Ministry A1 grammar, p.112](https://www.gsvetlly.minedu.gov.gr/publications/docs2023/ΟΔΥΣΣΕΑΣ_Εκμάθηση_της_Ελληνικής_γλώσσας_επίπεδο_Α1_-_Εγχειρίδιο.pdf). Masculine subject πόσοι differs from object πόσους; κόσμος remains grammatically singular.
- The teacher-object exercise teaches δασκάλους and also accepts the attested δάσκαλους variant: [CGL annotated corpus](https://pyli.greeklanguage.gr/index.php?catid=69&id=623&option=com_content&pageS=50&st=δα%&tab=1&view=article). No universal noun-stress rule is implied.
- Spelling variants αυγό/αβγό, φτηνός/φθηνός, φέτος/εφέτος and μοσχαρίσιος/μοσχαρήσιος are accepted. [CGL φέτος](https://www.greek-language.gr/greekLang/modern_greek/tools/lexica/triantafyllides/search.html?lq=φέτος), [μοσχαρίσιος variants and cited usage](https://el.wiktionary.org/wiki/μοσχαρίσιος).
- Παγάκι (ice cube) and τασάκι (ashtray) remain because they name distinct everyday objects. Other source plurals are represented by one base vocabulary card and, where needed, a new grammar objective.
- Πρόεδρος accepts either appropriate gender article; adjective vocabulary accepts its gender variants. The no-smoking construction stays in grammar, not first-person verb vocabulary.
- The permission-to-use-informal-address phrase retains polite σας while asking to switch to ενικός.
