# Greek words and component analysis — 25 November 2026

Sixteen existing words receive fuller Greek examples and component analysis in English, Russian and Chinese. Two words have duplicate source records, kept identical: αυτοκίνητο and κινηματογράφος. Only revealed explanations change; card identities, prompts, answers, dates and categories stay intact. Each locale receives an independent native-language review.

The notes translate added Greek forms, distinguish ancient words and bound components from modern usage, and preserve the earlier grammar, myths and quotations. Myth associations are not presented as established etymologies. The lesson skill now asks for useful original Greek and word analysis in future explanations.
