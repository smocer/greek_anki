# More vocabulary memory notes — 24 November 2026

Follow-up to the multilingual explanation review. The new notes cover 59 existing words in English, Russian and Chinese. They add short historical, cultural or word-family associations after the answer, preserving useful grammar and usage explanations. No vocabulary answers, categories, review identities or prompts are changed. Two repeated source definitions (car and cinema) use matching full explanations so the merged card shows its story once.

Each locale received an independent native-language editing pass across all selected notes. The table records the narrow factual basis and primary references. The card prose is original teaching text, not copied dictionary definitions. Some CGL pages rejected the browser fetch; their full HTML and etymology sections were checked through a direct read instead.

## Deliberate limits

Not every remaining word needs a story. The pass omits disputed food-invention legends, a certain Byblos-to-book naming direction, speculative April origins, and calendar folklore. Words absent as active standalone vocabulary (such as ψάρι and ελικόπτερο) were not added merely to attach trivia. Existing weekday, gift, catharsis and other earlier notes remain. The foreign-language route is stated where relevant: recognizable Greek roots do not prove that a modern term was inherited unchanged from antiquity.
